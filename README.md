# Bitcoin payments research

A public research notebook asking where demand for bitcoin payments could actually come from: which people, needs and situations self-custodial bitcoin payments could serve better than anything else. It's written by a designer building a bitcoin wallet. That perspective shapes which areas get studied, but the research itself stays neutral and doesn't recommend any product.

This is experimental, with no promise of ongoing maintenance.
