# Start here: where demand for bitcoin payments could come from

*A research notebook. Last updated November 10, 2026.*

Bitcoin payment technology works better than ever, yet few people pay with bitcoin. That gap was hard to miss at conferences throughout 2026. This library asks where demand could actually come from: which people, needs and situations self-custodial bitcoin payments could serve better than anything else.

## The working thesis

*Draft. It will be rewritten as the research comes in.*

What's missing is demand, and demand comes from products that serve real needs, not from protocols alone. Most of the ecosystem's attention goes to infrastructure. This library looks at people instead: what they do and make with money today, especially where existing payments serve them badly.

## What we've learned so far

*From quick scans of all areas and the first deep dives. To be tested.*

1. **People say they want bitcoin; they act on dollars.** Crypto salaries moved to stablecoins; in crisis economies, people spend in stablecoins and save in bitcoin.
2. **Bitcoin wins where something else carries the meaning:** a ritual, a community, or a real freeze. Low fees alone haven't been enough.
3. **Rituals spread payments socially:** receiving a gift makes people more likely to send one.
4. **In community money, the pain is bookkeeping and trust,** not the transfer itself.
5. **Consumers don't want agents to pay for them yet,** even with spending limits.
6. **The last mile is being built country by country,** for example phone numbers as Lightning addresses in Kenya.
